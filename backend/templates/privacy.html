Mainline alpha privacy notice

This is a temporary, invite-only alpha. The service may be unavailable, and account and repertoire data may be deleted when the alpha ends. Do not store anything here that you cannot safely lose.

Data Mainline stores

If you sign in with Lichess, Mainline stores your Lichess identity and an encrypted OAuth access token. It stores repertoires, profiles, opening notes and imported PGN annotations that you save, plus drill attempts and cached chess-position analysis. If you link Chess.com, it stores the public username you provide; this does not prove ownership of that account.

How data is used

The data is used only to provide and evaluate the alpha. Mainline calls Lichess and Chess.com to retrieve chess data for features you use. Those services process requests under their own privacy terms. Operational logs may contain technical request details and errors, but are not intended to contain OAuth tokens, cookies, PGN contents or repertoire notes.

Retention and deletion

The alpha database has no durability guarantee or managed backups. Data may disappear during testing or when the temporary database expires. To request deletion of your account and server-stored data, contact the person who invited you. Signed-out repertoire data remains only in your browser and can be removed by clearing this site's browser storage.

Questions

Contact the person who invited you before using the alpha if you have questions about this notice or are not comfortable with disposable test data.
